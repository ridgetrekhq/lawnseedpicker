Accuracy disclaimer

LawnSeedPicker provides general lawn-care guidance for educational purposes. Our recommendations draw on publicly available horticultural information from university extension programs, USDA fact sheets, and seed suppliers.

Lawns are local. Your actual results depend on conditions we can't see — your specific soil, microclimate, slope, drainage, existing pests, and how the lawn is cared for after seeding. Treat our recommendations as a well-informed starting point, not a guarantee.

Before buying, we encourage you to confirm seeding rates and product suitability with the seed supplier and the product label, and to check with your local cooperative extension office for guidance specific to your area. Where our sources disagree on a figure, we show a range rather than a single number, precisely because the right answer can vary.

LawnSeedPicker is not a substitute for professional agronomic or landscaping advice for high-stakes or commercial plantings.
