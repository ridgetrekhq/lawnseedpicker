Disclosure

Affiliate disclosure

LawnSeedPicker is free to use. We plan to keep it free by earning affiliate commissions from some of the seed retailers we link to.

That means: when the tool and articles include links to retailers, some of those will be affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a small commission. It costs you nothing extra — the price is the same whether you use our link or go to the retailer directly.

It does not change our recommendations

This is the part that matters. Which seed we recommend is driven by your conditions and by horticultural data from university extension programs and USDA sources — not by which retailer pays a commission, or how much. We don't accept payment to rank a product higher, and a retailer's commission rate has no bearing on whether or where a product appears in your results.

Why we disclose this clearly

The U.S. Federal Trade Commission requires that affiliate relationships be disclosed clearly and close to the links themselves, and we think that's the right thing to do regardless. You'll see a short disclosure near affiliate links on the pages where they appear, in addition to this page.
