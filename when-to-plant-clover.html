When to Plant Clover in Your Lawn

How the checker works

Your ZIP code is matched to a state or part of a state, using the same lookup as our grass seed calculator. Each area then gets the one source that speaks to it, quoted as written. Where that source gives dates, the checker compares them with today's date; where it gives a season or a month, the checker says so instead of making up dates. Where no extension source publishes a clover window for lawns in your area, the checker tells you that plainly.

Some sources were written for farm pastures, cover crops or bee lawns rather than ordinary lawns. The checker shows those as labelled extra notes, never as your main answer.

Why the advice differs by region

The extension sources point in different directions, and they were written for different places. In the Mid-Atlantic, University of Maryland Extension says the preferred time to seed a tall fescue and microclover lawn is "in the late summer to early fall". In the Piedmont of North Carolina, NC State Extension puts the best time for white clover in early fall, September 15 to October 15, and calls spring too late.

A University of Kentucky study that planted white clover into existing tall fescue and Kentucky bluegrass found the opposite pattern: of its April, July and October plantings, "The summer planting date yielded the highest WC plant numbers." In Minnesota, a University of Minnesota Extension answer says "The best time to seed is in the fall", with early spring second, and notes that the seed germinates in spring.

So there is no single right month for the whole country. If your area has a source, use that one.

If it's too late where you are

You have two fallbacks, and neither is a sure thing for lawns. Frost seeding clover scatters seed on frozen ground in late winter and lets freeze and thaw work it in; it only suits thin lawns on soil that truly freezes. Dormant seeding means sowing after the soil is too cold for seed to sprout, so it comes up in spring. Otherwise, wait for your next window and use the time to plan: how to overseed clover into an existing lawn and the clover seed calculator for how much to buy.

For whether clover suits your yard at all, start with clover lawns: it needs full sun, and its flowers draw bees.

Frequently asked questions

What is the latest you can plant clover?

It depends on where you are. In Maryland, University of Maryland Extension said that by early November it's "probably too late, yes". In the North Carolina Piedmont, NC State's best window ends October 15, though it says you can try frost seeding in February. Enter your ZIP above to see your own area's source.

Is October too late to plant clover?

Not everywhere. Early October falls inside NC State's September 15 to October 15 window for the North Carolina Piedmont. In the University of Kentucky study, though, the October planting was the weakest of the three dates, and the summer planting did best.

What month do you plant clover?

The sources disagree by region. Maryland's says late summer to early fall, North Carolina's says mid-September to mid-October, the Kentucky study found July worked best, and Minnesota's says fall, with early spring second. The checker above picks the one that applies to your ZIP.
